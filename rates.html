What's Included

Our hourly moving rates cover moving labour, moving blankets, straps, dollies, and the vehicle listed in the selected package. Depending on the service, our movers can assist with loading, unloading, transportation, furniture handling, and placement of your belongings.

Need more help? An additional mover can be added to any package for $60/hour, subject to the same booking minimum.

Important Pricing Information

2-Hour Minimum

All standard moving packages have a minimum charge of two hours. The minimum also applies when an additional helper is added to a booking.

Hourly Billing

Moving time is charged according to the hourly rate of the package selected. Your final cost may vary depending on the actual time required to complete the job.

Additional Travel

Standard rates apply to local Calgary services. Travel outside Calgary may include an additional travel charge.

Disposal Fees

Junk removal pricing includes labour. Landfill, disposal, dumping, or other facility charges are additional and will be based on the applicable receipt or disposal fee.

Packing Materials

Boxes, wrapping materials, tape, protective materials, and other packing supplies are charged separately when requested or used.

Specialty Items

Pianos, safes, unusually heavy items, oversized furniture, and other specialty moves may require a customized quote.

Long-Distance Moves

Long-distance moving is available by customized quotation based on distance, truck requirements, labour, travel time, and the size of the move.
