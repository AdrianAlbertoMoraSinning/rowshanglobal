About Rowshan

A local moving company focused on dependable service and careful handling.

Rowshan Moving Company was established in 2025 to provide practical moving and transport support for households and businesses throughout Calgary and nearby communities.

Built around responsibility, communication and respect.

Owner Lal Mohammad Rowshan created the company with a simple service standard: arrive prepared, communicate clearly and treat every customer’s property with care.

The service range covers residential and small-business moves, difficult items, furniture setup, packing help, local deliveries and removal runs.

Our service priorities

  • Courteous and prepared service
  • Protection-focused handling
  • Organized loading and unloading
  • Clear estimates and invoicing
  • Responsive customer communication
