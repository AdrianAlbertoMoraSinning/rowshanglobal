About Rowshan Moving Company

Moving Made Simple, Careful, and Reliable

Rowshan Moving Company (R.M.C.) is a Calgary-based moving service committed to making every move organized, efficient, and less stressful.

Whether you are moving a few items, an apartment, a house, or a business, our team approaches every job with preparation, care, and respect. We understand that moving is not only about transporting belongings; it is about helping people begin a new stage of their lives with confidence and peace of mind.

From the first conversation to the final placement of your belongings, we work to provide clear communication, dependable service, and careful handling.

Who We Are

Established in Calgary in 2025, Rowshan Moving Company was created to provide customers with honest, practical, and professional moving support.

We serve individuals, families, seniors, students, property managers, and businesses throughout Calgary and surrounding communities. Our services are flexible and can be customized for small deliveries, complete moves, labour-only assistance, and specialized moving requirements.

No two moves are exactly the same. That is why we take time to understand your needs, explain the available options, and help you select the appropriate movers, vehicle, and services for your job.

Our Mission

Safe, reliable and customer-focused moving services.

Our mission is to provide safe, reliable, and customer-focused moving services through careful planning, respectful communication, responsible handling, and transparent pricing.

We aim to make every customer feel informed, supported and confident throughout the moving process.

Our Vision

A trusted and respected moving company in Calgary.

Our vision is to become a trusted and respected moving company in Calgary, recognized for reliability, professionalism, careful service, and long-term customer relationships.

We want Rowshan Moving Company to be a name that customers confidently recommend to their families, friends, neighbours and businesses.

Our Core Values

How we approach every move.

Reliability

We understand the importance of arriving prepared and completing the work responsibly. We respect our customers’ time and do our best to provide dependable service from booking to completion.

Care

We handle customers’ furniture and belongings as carefully as possible. Protective equipment and appropriate moving methods are used based on the needs of the job.

Professionalism

We maintain respectful communication, organized service and a professional approach throughout every stage of the move.

Honesty and Transparency

We explain our rates, minimum booking requirements, and possible additional charges before the move. Customers receive clear information so they can make informed decisions.

Customer Satisfaction

We listen to our customers and work to provide a positive experience. Our goal is not simply to complete a move but to earn trust and build lasting relationships.

Why Choose Rowshan Moving Company?

Flexible Moving Options

We offer labour-only service, pickup-truck packages and different box-truck options. This allows customers to choose a package suited to the size and requirements of their move.

Clear and Transparent Pricing

Our standard hourly rates and two-hour minimum are explained before booking. Additional labour, travel, packing materials, disposal fees, and specialty-item charges are discussed when applicable.

Careful Handling

We approach every move carefully and use suitable moving equipment based on the job requirements. This may include dollies, moving blankets, straps, and other protective materials.

Complete Moving Support

Customers may request help with packing, loading, transportation, unloading, furniture placement, assembly, and other related services.

Local Knowledge

Personalized Service
