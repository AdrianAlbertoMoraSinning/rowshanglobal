Who we are

Honest, hardworking and stress-free moving support.

Established in 2025, Rowshan Moving Company is a Calgary-based moving company serving families and businesses across Calgary and surrounding areas.

Built on trust and careful work.

Founded by Lal Mohammad Rowshan, the company is built around punctuality, clear communication and careful handling of customers’ belongings.

Customers rely on the team for full-service moves, heavy-item handling, furniture assembly, packing assistance, deliveries and junk removal.

What makes us different?

  • Careful furniture and fragile item handling
  • Efficient loading and unloading
  • Transparent pricing
  • Strong communication
